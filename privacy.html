Privacy Policy

Pocket Arcade Database — last updated November 25, 2026

Pocket Arcade Database is a free, hobbyist-run reference site listing arcade games playable on the Analogue Pocket. Browsing the database requires no account and involves no tracking, advertising, or analytics.

Accounts (optional)

You may create an account to sync your personal game lists across devices. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth, either with an email magic link or with Google / GitHub single sign-on. When you sign in, we receive and store:

  • your email address,
  • a unique account identifier,
  • the lists you create (game selections and your personal notes on them).

If you sign in with Google or GitHub, those providers confirm your identity to us; we never see or store your password for those services.

Local storage

If you use the site without an account, your lists and display preferences are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and never leave your device.

How your data is used

Your email address and lists are used solely to provide the sign-in and list-sync features. Your data is never sold, shared with third parties for marketing, or used for advertising.

Data hosting

Account data is stored with Supabase (database hosting). The site itself is served by GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

You can delete your lists at any time from within the site. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact the maintainer via GitHub: github.com/syltendo.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.
